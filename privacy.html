Privacy policy

This app is a private tool used only by its owner, with their own Google account.

  • It requests read-only Gmail access. It cannot send, delete or change email.
  • It reads receipts and order emails to keep the owner's own records, which are stored privately.
  • Item descriptions and receipt images may be processed by an AI service to extract details. Nothing else is shared with third parties.
  • Data is not sold, shared, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
